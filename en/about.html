About DITM Lab

We conduct data-driven research on digital transformation, AI-enabled management, and technology management.

DITM Lab investigates how digital transformation and AI adoption shape decision-making and performance for organizations, markets, and individuals. We use econometrics and empirical methods to address meaningful questions from industry practice.

Digital Transformation

We analyze how digital transformation in industries and organizations affects performance and competition.

AI Management

We study the mechanisms through which AI adoption influences decision-making, productivity, and capability development.

Technology Management and Entrepreneurship

We examine the diffusion and commercialization of technological innovation, entrepreneurship, and innovation ecosystems.
