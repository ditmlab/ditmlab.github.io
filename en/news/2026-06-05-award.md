## Award announcement

A paper by PhD student **Haewon Sim** and master’s student **Soobin Kim** received an **Outstanding Paper Award at the 2026 Joint Spring Conference on Management Information Systems**, held at K Hotel in Gyeongju.

![Outstanding Paper Award presentation](/assets/img/20260605_award_1.jpeg)
![DITM Lab members at the conference](/assets/img/20260605_award_2.jpeg)

### Paper and award

- Paper: “The Effect of Platform AI Disclosure Policies on Content Supply Concentration: Evidence from the AI Cover Market”
- Conference: 2026 Joint Spring Conference on Management Information Systems
- Award: Outstanding Paper Award

Congratulations on the award!
