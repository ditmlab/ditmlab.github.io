## Award announcement

PhD student **Haewon Sim** received the **YoungLimWon Soft Lab Best Paper Award**. The paper was selected as the best paper among those published in the second half of 2025 in *Information Systems Review*.

![Best Paper Award announcement](/assets/img/20251101_award.png)

### Paper and award

- Paper: “Changes in User Questioning Behavior on Q&A Platforms Following the Introduction of Generative AI: A Focus on Utilitarian Keywords”
- Journal: Information Systems Review
- Award: YoungLimWon Soft Lab Best Paper Award

Congratulations on this achievement!
