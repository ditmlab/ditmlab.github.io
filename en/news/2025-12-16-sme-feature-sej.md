A paper coauthored by Professor Namil Kim was featured on **Strategic Management Explorer**, the blog of the **Strategic Management Society (SMS)**, on December 16, 2025.

### Featured paper

- Paper: *Anti-labor environments and employee entrepreneurship: Evidence from right-to-work laws*
- Journal: **Strategic Entrepreneurship Journal** (FT50)
- [Read the paper](https://doi.org/10.1002/sej.70006)

### About the feature

The article discusses how institutional changes that weaken union protections, such as the adoption of right-to-work laws in the United States, can relate to workers’ transitions into self-employment. It also highlights differences across groups and implications for firms and policymakers.

[Read the Strategic Management Explorer article](https://www.strategicmanagement.net/publications-resources/strategic-management-explorer/without-union-protections-workers-start-their-own-businesses/).
