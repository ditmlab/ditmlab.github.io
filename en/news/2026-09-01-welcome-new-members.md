## New lab members

Two new researchers have joined DITM Lab for the Fall 2026 semester.

**Seunggyu Go** joins as a PhD student. Research interests include AI transformation, business management, human–AI interaction, and agentic AI.

**Yeonwoo Kim** joins as a master’s student. Research interests include AI and digital transformation, human decision-making, and collaboration with agentic AI.

All lab members look forward to supporting their research. Welcome! 🎉

We are excited to continue learning and growing together.
