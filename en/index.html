Research Areas

We use large-scale data, computational methods, and rigorous empirical analysis to understand the societal and organizational impacts of AI and technological change.

DT

Digital Transformation

We study transformation strategies and performance mechanisms in organizations and industries.

AI

AI Management

We analyze how AI adoption affects decision-making, productivity, and organizational capabilities.

TM

Technology and Innovation

We explore innovation diffusion, commercialization, entrepreneurship, and ecosystems.
